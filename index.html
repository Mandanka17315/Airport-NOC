How the check works. The zone is a runway-aligned rectangle (~2.48 km × ~1.32 km). Your point is tested against the actual polygon corners using a ray-casting geometry check, and the distance to the nearest edge is measured on the ground.

Zone corners (WGS 84): NW 22.33186, 73.21080 · NE 22.34177, 73.23239 · SE 22.33086, 73.23739 · SW 22.32096, 73.21580.

Accuracy. Corners were georeferenced from the CCZM map against the runway ends, so expect roughly ±50–100 m at the boundary. Phone GPS adds its own error (shown as accuracy). Near the edge, treat the result as indicative and verify against the official CCZM drawing AAI/25-B-CCZM(GRID)/VADODARA/2016. This tool does not replace an official AAI determination.
