Position relative to all zones

Rings from centre out: Red (NOC required) · Grey 60 m · Yellow 80 m · Violet 100 m · Green 120 m · Blue 140 m permissible top elevation (AMSL).

Zone data. Red zone corners (WGS 84): NW 22.33186, 73.21080 · NE 22.34177, 73.23239 · SE 22.33086, 73.23739 · SW 22.32096, 73.21580. The five outer colour bands are modelled as rings centred on the Airport Reference Point (22.33004, 73.21884) at the radii printed on the official CCZM drawing: 2250 m / 4000 m / 6100 m / 9100 m / 15000 m.

Accuracy. The red zone was georeferenced directly from the CCZM raster (±50–100 m). The outer rings are a radial approximation of AAI's true (slightly irregular, grid-cell-based) boundaries — reliable well inside or outside a band, less exact within ~150 m of a ring edge. For any construction proposal, confirm the applicable height limit against the official drawing AAI/25-B-CCZM(GRID)/VADODARA/2016 or with the AAI NOC cell before proceeding. This tool is a field aid, not an official determination.
